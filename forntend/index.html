About Pulse

Pulse reads day-to-day academic signals — attendance, marks, submissions, financial background — and turns them into an early warning system for educational institutions.

Early Intervention

Designed to help mentors step in weeks before a student disengages permanently, reducing institutional drop-out rates proactively.

Data-Driven Metrics

Focuses on core indicators such as academic performance, submission compliance, household background, and financial status.

Privacy First

Built strictly for early support and counselling workflows rather than continuous surveillance or punitive tracking.
